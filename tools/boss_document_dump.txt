[259] Our Services
[260] OUR SERVICES
[261] 
[262] Comprehensive Poultry Farming & Agricultural Solutions
[263] 
[264] At Busia Chicken Limited, we are committed to delivering quality poultry products and sustainable agricultural solutions. Our operations cover egg production, poultry farming, animal feeds manufacturing, organic manure production, organic foliar fertilizers, chicken meat production and poultry farming consultancy. We combine modern farming practices, innovation and expertise to support farmers, businesses and consumers.
[265] 
[266] 1. Egg Production
[267] 
[268] We specialize in the production and supply of high-quality, farm-fresh eggs. Through proper flock management, quality nutrition and strict biosecurity measures, we ensure consistent production of nutritious eggs that meet the needs of households, retailers, hotels and other businesses.
[269] 
[270] 2. Organic Manure Production
[271] 
[272] We produce organic manure from poultry waste, transforming bird droppings and other poultry remains into valuable organic fertilizer. Our manure improves soil fertility, enhances soil structure and supports sustainable crop production while promoting environmentally responsible waste management.
[273] 
[274] 3. Organic Folia Production
[275] 
[276] We produce Organic Folia, an organic agricultural product designed to support plant growth and improve crop productivity. Our focus is on providing sustainable agricultural solutions that help farmers improve crop performance and promote environmentally friendly farming practices.
[277] 
[278] 4. Animal Feeds Production
[279] 
[280] We manufacture quality poultry feeds formulated to support healthy growth, improved productivity and optimal bird performance. Our feeds cater to different poultry growth stages, providing essential nutrients for broilers, layers and other poultry breeds while promoting efficient and affordable poultry farming.
[281] 
[282] 5. Chicken Farming Consultancy
[283] 
[284] We provide professional poultry farming consultancy to individuals, farmers and agricultural businesses looking to establish, manage or expand their poultry farming operations. Our consultancy covers farm planning, housing, breed selection, feeding programs, disease prevention, flock management, biosecurity and production optimization.
[285] 
[286] 6. Chicken Meat Production
[287] 
[288] We engage in chicken meat production, supplying quality poultry meat to households, hotels, restaurants, retailers and other consumers. Through proper bird management, quality feeding and responsible farming practices, we aim to provide healthy, fresh and quality chicken products.
